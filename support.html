Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Q: Where is my data stored? Can it be lost?

Q：我的数据存在哪里？会丢失吗？

All habit and check-in data is stored only on your own device and never uploaded to a server. Uninstalling the app or clearing local storage deletes the data. Pro users are advised to back up regularly with "data export."

所有习惯与打卡数据只存在你自己的设备本地，不上传服务器。卸载或清除本地存储会删除数据。建议专业版用户定期使用"数据导出"备份。

Q: How does the 7-day free trial work? Will it auto-charge?

Q：7 天免费试用怎么算？会自动扣费吗？

During the trial you have all Pro features, no payment method required, and no automatic charge. After the 7-day countdown ends, you revert to the free plan; to keep using Pro features, purchase the one-time unlock manually.

试用期内你拥有专业版全部功能，无需绑定支付、不会自动扣费。7 天倒计时结束后自动回退免费版，如需继续使用专业版功能，再手动买断即可。

Q: After changing phones / reinstalling, is my Pro purchase still there?

Q：换手机 / 重装后，买断的专业版还在吗？

In the App Store, you can find your Pro entitlement again via the "restore purchases" feature in the app — no need to pay twice. Losing a local unlock flag does not affect your purchase record on Apple's servers.

在 App Store 内，可通过应用内"恢复购买"找回专业版权益，无需重复付费。本地解锁标记丢失不影响你在苹果服务器侧的购买记录。

Q: Can I get a refund?

Q：可以退款吗？

The one-time purchase is settled through the Apple App Store, and refunds follow Apple policy. If a purchase went wrong or you have questions about an order, email 365504860@qq.com.

一次性买断通过 Apple App Store 结算，退款按 Apple 政策处理。如购买异常或对订单有疑问，可邮件 365504860@qq.com 说明。

Q: Will you use my data for ads?

Q：你们会拿我的数据做广告吗？

No. Seeora has no accounts, no backend, and no third-party tracking or ad SDKs; your data never leaves your device.

不会。Seeora 无账号、无服务端、无第三方追踪与广告 SDK，你的数据从不离开设备。
